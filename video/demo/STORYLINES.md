# Ashgrave how-to-play video: three storylines

The one thing: **you can solve a murder in about 15 minutes by searching, testing, catching lies and naming the killer, with AI suspects who live in your team's chat.**

Every screen shown is captured from the real running game (localhost:5180) and Mattermost. Nothing is drawn as a fake UI.

## A. One case, start to finish (recommended)

- **Device / form:** a worked example as an object-to-object chain. One detective plays the real case, and each shot ends on the thing that opens the next one.
- **What the viewer sees:** a glint becomes the found card. The card drops onto the lab bench. The flask's glow lights the burned letter. The letter's words become the evidence dragged onto Dr. Vale's lie. The viewer learns every verb of the game by watching one case get solved, and feels the click of the LIE CAUGHT stamp.
- **First 2 s:** rain over the painted conservatory, with a black orchid in a still hand. The line *"Lady Verity is dead. Find out who, how and why."*
- **Beats:** hook → three-chapter goals → lantern search → lab test (wrong guess fizzles, right one glows) → letter puzzle → drag the gate ledger onto Vale's lie, LIE stamp → stuck? hint from the ghost → evidence board, red string draws → three picks → GUILTY and the reveal panel → a two-second split showing every move landing in Mattermost through Switch → end card.
- **Look:** the game's own world: candlelit ink-black, parchment, oxblood stamps, IM Fell English titles. Real screens shown large and pushed in on what the cursor touches.
- **Sound:** a rain bed under a low string-and-piano pulse. A chime on finds, a soft thud on a wrong guess, a stamp hit on lies, and a church bell on each chapter.
- **Length:** about 60–75 s.

## B. Two screens, one game

- **Device / form:** one object across apps, split screen collapsing to one. The game window sits on the left and Mattermost #conservatory on the right.
- **What the viewer sees:** every click on the left appears on the right as a chat message, and the AI suspects answer in both places. A friend types in chat and the game updates. It proves Switch is the hub, then collapses into the game for the finale.
- **First 2 s:** the split screen. A click on a glint (left) and `@game-master find orchid` popping into chat (right) at the same instant.
- **Beats:** join → search mirrored → lab mirrored → lie caught in both → a teammate plays from chat → accusation → panes collapse into GUILTY → end card.
- **Look:** the game's palette on the left, Mattermost's own light UI on the right, with a candle-gold seam between them.
- **Sound:** rain bed, soft key clicks for chat posts, the same stamp and bell hits.
- **Length:** about 55–65 s.

## C. The detective's desk (bold)

- **Device / form:** a flight through one world. A detective's corkboard desk where each how-to-play step is a pinned photo of a real screen. Red string leads the camera from pin to pin.
- **What the viewer sees:** the camera follows the string across the desk. Each pinned screenshot springs to life as it is reached, then the string pulls taut to the next one. It ends on the GUILTY stamp pressed onto Vale's polaroid.
- **First 2 s:** a macro shot of a red pin as the string snaps taut, then the camera rides the string to the lantern-search photo.
- **Beats:** pin 1 search → pin 2 lab → pin 3 letter → pin 4 lie → pin 5 board → pin 6 accuse → stamp → end card.
- **Look:** cork, brass pins, oxblood string, polaroids of the real UI.
- **Sound:** rain, pin taps, string twangs, a final stamp.
- **Length:** about 45–55 s.
